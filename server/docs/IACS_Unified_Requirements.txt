PREFACE
=============================================================================
IACS Unified Requirements (URs) are mandatory requirements binding on all IACS member societies (LR, DNV, ABS, BV, CCS, GL, KR, NK, RINA, RS, IRS). Where a UR applies, all member societies shall include it in their rules without modification. URs are identified by a letter series prefix (S = Strength, W = Welding, M = Machinery, E = Electrical, P = Pollution Prevention, Z = General, I = IMO-related).

=============================================================================
S-SERIES — STRUCTURAL STRENGTH
=============================================================================

UR S1 — Anchor Windlass and Mooring Equipment
S1.1 Anchor windlass shall be capable of lifting the anchor and chain from a depth of not less than 82.5 m at a mean speed of not less than 9 m/min.
S1.2 Windlass motor continuous rating shall equal or exceed the pull required for 3 m/min operation.
S1.3 Brake holding capacity: not less than 1.4 × static chain tension at maximum rated pull.
S1.4 Wildcat (gypsy): must hold 75% of the minimum breaking load (MBL) of the chain cable.
S1.5 Chain stopper: rated at 80% MBL of the chain cable. Chain stopper and windlass foundations designed for a load of 1.5 × MBL.
S1.6 Anchor chain lockers: capacity for full rated chain. Painted and protected. Spurling pipe with closing plate/cover to prevent flooding.

UR S2 — Corrugated Bulkheads
S2.1 Corrugated bulkheads are subject to the same design pressure as flat plate bulkheads.
S2.2 Effective section modulus of corrugated panel (per unit width) = (b × d² × t) / (6 × c) where b = flange breadth, d = corrugation depth, t = plate thickness, c = corrugation pitch.
S2.3 Trough radius r ≥ 3 × t to prevent weld cracking at corrugation troughs.
S2.4 Shedder plate at top of corrugation to prevent water ponding.

UR S6 — Use of Steel Grades
S6.1 The selection of steel grades is governed by the structural member category and the plate thickness (see table below).
S6.2 Structural member categories:
  I — Special Category: Strength deck stringer plates in the midship 0.4L; sheer strakes adjacent to strength deck at amidships; bilge strakes in the midship half-length where t > 25 mm; inner bottom strakes next to bilge at amidships; horizontal girder strakes.
  II — Primary Category: Strength deck plating within 0.1B of centreline amidships; side shell and longitudinal bulkhead plating within 0.1B of deck.
  III — Secondary Category: All other members not in I or II.
S6.3 Minimum grade requirements for normal strength steel (235 N/mm² yield):
  t ≤ 15 mm: A (all categories)
  15 < t ≤ 20 mm: A (III), A (II), A (I)
  20 < t ≤ 25 mm: A (III), B (II), B (I)
  25 < t ≤ 30 mm: A (III), D (II), D (I)
  30 < t ≤ 35 mm: A (III), D (II), E (I)
  35 < t ≤ 40 mm: B (III), E (II), E (I)
  t > 40 mm: B (III), E (II), E (I)
S6.4 For high-tensile steel: corresponding AH/DH/EH/FH grades with identical temperature mapping.
S6.5 Z-grade through-thickness steel: required at all highly restrained connections where t > 50 mm and calculated through-thickness stress σ_z exceeds 50 N/mm². Minimum Z25 (ZC designation — 25% reduction in area on through-thickness tensile test).

UR S7 — Minimum Longitudinal Strength (Hull Girder Section Modulus)
S7.1 The required midship section modulus to the strength deck (SM_D) and to the keel (SM_K) shall be:
  SM_D, SM_K ≥ 0.01 × C_W × L² × B × (4.1 × C_b + 0.7) cm³
  where C_b ≥ 0.6; C_W as defined in the Classification Society rules.
S7.2 This represents the absolute minimum — Classification Society rules may require larger values based on loading conditions and sea area.

UR S11 — Longitudinal Strength Standard
S11.1 The vertical wave bending moment M_WV at amidships:
  Hogging (+): M_WV,H = +0.19 × C_W × L^2 × B × (C_b + 0.7) × 10^-3 MN·m
  Sagging (-): M_WV,S = -0.11 × C_W × L^2 × B × (C_b + 0.7) × 10^-3 MN·m
S11.2 Wave coefficient C_W:
  C_W = 10.75 − ((300-L)/100)^1.5 for 90 ≤ L < 300 m
  C_W = 10.75 for 300 ≤ L ≤ 350 m
  C_W = 10.75 − ((L-350)/150)^1.5 for 350 < L ≤ 500 m
S11.3 Combined vertical bending moment at amidships = SWBM + M_WV (algebraically for both hogging and sagging).
S11.4 Permissible stress in hull girder section:
  Normal condition σ_p = 175/k N/mm²; in port condition σ_p = 235/k N/mm².
  k = (235/ReH); for mild steel k = 1.0.

UR S17 — Environmental Exposure for Corrosion Protection
S17.1 Protective coating systems for ballast water tanks: IACS UR S17 mandates qualification of coating system per IMO PSPC (Performance Standard for Protective Coatings) for ballast tanks and void spaces of vessels ≥ 500 GT.
S17.2 Coating system shall achieve minimum protection life of 5 years in PSPC type approval testing.
S17.3 All ballast water tanks and void spaces to be coated prior to closing. IRS/Class surveyor to certify coating application.

UR S18 — Anchor and Chain Cables
S18.1 Equipment number EN for anchor and chain: EN = Δ^(2/3) + 2 × B × H_E + A/10
  where Δ = displacement (tonnes), B = breadth (m), H_E = freeboard + sheer amidships, A = projected lateral area of hull above waterline (m²).
S18.2 Number and mass of bower anchors and chain length per EN lookup table.
S18.3 Chain grade for bow anchors: Grade K2 minimum for all vessels. Grade K3 for high EN or ice class.
S18.4 Chain stud minimum: 27% of chain diameter. Chain links tested in proof load and breaking load.

UR S21 — Hatch Cover Design for Bulk Carriers
S21.1 Design load for hatch covers on exposed freeboard deck:
  Forward 0.25L: p = 49.0 kN/m²
  0.25L to 0.5L: p = 34.3 kN/m²
  Aft of 0.5L: p = 24.5 kN/m²
S21.2 Hatch cover permissible stress: σ_allow = ReH/1.35. Deflection ≤ l/200 (l = span).
S21.3 Hatch coaming plate minimum: 9.0 mm; hatch coaming stay minimum 8.0 mm.
S21.4 Tightness test: hose test after each installation, ultrasonic test at each annual survey.

UR S22 — Design of Watertight Closing Appliances
S22.1 All watertight closing appliances (doors, hatches, covers, valves, pipe penetrations) on the subdivision deck or below shall be capable of withstanding the design head of water.
S22.2 Test: hydraulic test to design head. Hose test accepted for door framing and hatch covers.
S22.3 Indicators: position indicators (open/closed) at the appliance and remote display at bridge/control for all watertight doors below the waterline.

W-SERIES — WELDING
=============================================================================

UR W0 — Approval of Welding Consumables
W0.1 All welding consumables (electrodes, wires, fluxes) used in ship hull construction shall be type-approved by a Classification Society. Multi-lateral agreement: approval by one IACS member is accepted by all others.
W0.2 Consumable grades:
  - Grade 1: yield ≥ 305 N/mm², tensile 400–560, Charpy 47J at 0°C. For Grade A steel.
  - Grade 2: yield ≥ 375 N/mm², tensile 490–660, Charpy 47J at 0°C. For Grade B steel.
  - Grade 3: yield ≥ 375 N/mm², tensile 490–660, Charpy 47J at -20°C. For Grade D steel.
  - Grade 4: yield ≥ 375 N/mm², tensile 490–660, Charpy 47J at -40°C. For Grade E steel.
W0.3 HY consumables (H2 grades) for high-tensile steel: additional diffusible hydrogen test. Max 5 ml H2 per 100 g weld metal (H5 classification).

UR W7 — Approval of Welders
W7.1 Welders shall hold current certification for the applicable welding process, position, and thickness range. Certificate shall not be more than 2 years old (or 3 years if renewal NDT test passed within 6 months).
W7.2 Certification per: ISO 9606-1 (steel); ISO 9606-2 (aluminium); or ASME Section IX.
W7.3 Classification Society may require re-test if quality of welder's work is found unsatisfactory.
W7.4 Welder log: each welder's certifications, work history on the vessel, and test results shall be recorded and available to surveyor.

UR W11 — Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) of Welds
W11.1 NDT methods accepted: visual testing (VT); radiographic testing (RT); ultrasonic testing (UT); magnetic particle testing (MT); liquid penetrant testing (PT).
W11.2 Mandatory NDT coverage for hull structures:
  - Keel plate butt welds: 100% RT or UT.
  - Butt welds in shell plating below waterline: 100% UT or RT.
  - Butt welds in strength deck plating within midship 0.4L: minimum 10% UT or RT; 100% at surveyor discretion.
  - All full-penetration T-welds in primary structure (floors to girders, frames to keel): 5% UT.
  - Butt welds in watertight bulkheads: 5% RT or UT.
  - Weld repairs: 100% NDT on each repair.
W11.3 NDT acceptance standards: per approved standard (AWS D1.1, ASME Section V + VIII, EN ISO 11666 for UT, EN 1435 for RT, or equivalent).
W11.4 RT interpretation: density range 2.3–4.0 OD. IQI sensitivity minimum 2%. Geometric unsharpness Ug ≤ 0.5 mm.
W11.5 UT angle probes: 45°, 60°, 70° refraction angles. DAC curve established from 3 mm flat-bottom hole reference or equivalent. Discontinuities above 100% DAC are not acceptable.

UR W17 — Approval of Welding Procedures
W17.1 All structural welding processes shall be qualified per IACS UR W17. WPS (Welding Procedure Specification) and PQR (Procedure Qualification Record) required.
W17.2 Test welds: deposited per WPS conditions. Test pieces: tensile, bend (root, face), Charpy V-notch (if grade D or above base metal), and macrosection.
W17.3 Hardness testing: Vickers HV10 traverse across HAZ and weld metal. Maximum hardness 350 HV10 for carbon-manganese steel.
W17.4 Qualification scope: base metal grade, thickness range (±20% of test thickness), welding position, joint type (butt, T, corner), heat input range (±20% of test HI), preheat minimum.
W17.5 Pre-heat requirements (minimum): Grade A/B t > 25 mm: 50°C; Grade D/E: 75°C; HT steel t > 20 mm: 100°C. Measure with contact thermometer or crayon at 75 mm from weld line.

UR W28 — Welding Procedure Tests
W28.1 Supplements W17 with specific requirements for welding of special steels (Grade F, Z-grade, clad steel) and special processes (electroslag, friction stir, laser hybrid).
W28.2 Z-grade welding: procedure test must include through-thickness tensile test of HAZ to confirm Z properties maintained after welding. Minimum RA ≥ 10% in HAZ.

M-SERIES — MACHINERY
=============================================================================

UR M56 — Crankshaft Design for Diesel Engines
M56.1 Minimum crankpin and journal diameters for main propulsion diesel engines per ICS/IACS UR M56 formula. Based on cylinder bore, stroke, and rated power.
M56.2 Crankshaft material: forged steel, minimum UTS 600 N/mm², yield 360 N/mm², Charpy 27J at 0°C.
M56.3 Fatigue assessment of crankshaft: safety factor on fatigue limit ≥ 1.5.
M56.4 Critical speed calculation: torsional vibration resonance to be avoided ±10% of operating range. Detuner or vibration damper required if resonance cannot be avoided.

UR M57 — Shaft Alignment for Propulsion
M57.1 Propulsion shaft alignment calculation required for all shafts L_shaft > 4 × D_shaft. Includes static deflection of intermediate and propeller shaft bearings.
M57.2 Bearing loads to be within manufacturer recommendations over the full range of ship loading (light ship to full load) and temperature range.
M57.3 Alignment verification: jack-up test or strain gauge measurement at installation. Acceptable criterion: bearing reaction loads within ±20% of calculated values.
M57.4 Offset and angle at couplings: offset ≤ 0.05 mm, angular misalignment ≤ 0.05 mm/100 mm coupling diameter.
M57.5 Oil film thickness in stern tube bearing (for oil-lubricated type): minimum 0.5 μm at worst operating condition per hydrodynamic analysis.

UR M72 — Engine Room Manning — Periodically Unattended Machinery Spaces (UMS)
M72.1 Ships with UMS notation shall have automated monitoring and control allowing engine room operation without continuous manual attendance.
M72.2 Required alarms for UMS: high water in bilge; fuel oil high temperature; lube oil low pressure; cooling water high temperature; bearing temperature high; shaft speed limit; fire in machinery space; main engine automatic shutdown.
M72.3 Bridge control: main engine speed and direction changeable from bridge without engine room attendance. Telegraph and direct propulsion controls.
M72.4 Deadman system: engineer alarm triggered if no response within 30 minutes.

E-SERIES — ELECTRICAL
=============================================================================

UR E1 — Testing of Automatic and Remote Control Systems
E1.1 Control systems shall be tested in all operational modes prior to delivery. Tests include: automatic start, stop, and load transfer; response to simulated failures; stability under normal and abnormal loads.

UR E10 — Electrical Installations — General
E10.1 See IEC 60092 series for detailed electrical requirements. IACS UR E10 confirms IEC 60092 is the mandatory standard for electrical installations on ships classed with IACS members.
E10.2 Emergency power supply for ships ≥ 500 GT:
  - Emergency generator: start within 30 seconds (45 seconds for ships < 500 GT).
  - Autonomy: minimum 18 hours for cargo ships (SOLAS III-2).
  - Supplies: emergency lighting, fire detection and alarm, general alarm, navigation lights, VHF, GMDSS.
E10.3 Insulation resistance: all electrical circuits minimum 1 MΩ at 500 V megger before energisation.

P-SERIES — POLLUTION PREVENTION
=============================================================================

UR P1 — Bilge Systems
P1.1 Every ship shall have an efficient bilge pumping system capable of pumping from and draining any watertight compartment.
P1.2 Bilge pump capacity: Q_min = 2.0 × (2d + D) × L / 1000 litres/min
  where d = internal bilge suction pipe diameter (mm), D = bilge main diameter (mm), L = ship length (m).
P1.3 Minimum number of power-driven bilge pumps:
  - Ships 24–90 m: 2 pumps (one may be the general service pump).
  - Ships 90–120 m: 2 bilge pumps + 1 bilge injector (fire or ballast pump).
  - Ships > 120 m: 3 bilge pumps with at least 1 independent power source.
P1.4 Emergency bilge suction from engine room bilge: direct suction connection on the largest installed pump. Emergency suction pipe diameter minimum 50 mm. Operating valve accessible and labelled.
P1.5 Non-return valves on all bilge suctions to prevent flooding of compartment from overboard.
P1.6 Bilge suction in each watertight compartment. Suction bell-mouth within 150 mm of floor.

UR P2 — Oil-Water Separator (OWS) and 15 ppm Monitoring
P2.1 Ships ≥ 400 GT: oil-water separator (OWS) fitted with automatic stopping device. OWS type-approved to IMO Resolution MEPC.107(49) or later.
P2.2 OWS performance: effluent ≤ 15 ppm oil in water. Automatic shut-off: stops overboard discharge when oil content meter reads ≥ 15 ppm.
P2.3 15 ppm alarm/automatic stopping device wired directly to bilge pump overboard discharge valve motor circuit. Manual override not permitted for routine discharge.
P2.4 Oil record book (ORB Part I) to record all bilge operations: date, time, ship position, quantity, method of disposal, officer signature.

Z-SERIES — GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================================

UR Z7 — Periodically Unattended Machinery Spaces (see M72)
UR Z9 — Test Requirements for Fire Dampers
Z9.1 Fire dampers fitted in ventilation ducts passing through Class A or B fire divisions shall be type-tested per IMO Resolution A.754(18) or ISO 10294.
Z9.2 Temperature-sensitive elements (fusible links) shall be replaced at each annual survey or when operated.
Z9.3 Remote release and remote indicator required for all dampers serving machinery spaces.

UR Z10 — Conditions of Classification
Z10.1 Annual survey: general examination of all structural and machinery components.
Z10.2 Intermediate survey (2.5 years): in-water or dry-dock survey of hull below waterline, rudder, stern gear.
Z10.3 Special survey (5 years): full dry-dock survey. Thickness measurement of all structural members. Non-destructive testing as required.
Z10.4 Thickness measurement plan: required for all ships after 10 years of age. Suspect areas to be gauged at every special survey.
Z10.5 Conditions for suspending classification: failure to complete required surveys; non-conformities not rectified within agreed time; structural or machinery damage affecting seaworthiness.

UR Z23 — Annual Survey of Machinery
Z23.1 All main propulsion and auxiliary machinery to be examined at annual survey.
Z23.2 Boiler internal and external survey every 2.5 years or aligned with intermediate hull survey.
Z23.3 Safety valves on boilers and pressure vessels: tested at each annual survey (cold set check + blow-through where practicable).
Z23.4 Main engine crankcase: internal examination at 2.5-year intervals or as required by condition monitoring.
